# Palimpsest Product Roadmap

## Step 1 — Discovery
- Archive search, filters, saved records, comparison.
- Curated collections and chronology browsing.

## Step 2 — Research layer
- Document library.
- Local research notebook.
- Markdown export for notes and internal guidance.

## Step 3 — Digital preservation
- Installable shell and offline cache.
- Immutable raw-scan principle.
- Evidence labels for observed, inferred, and speculative content.

## Step 4 — Backend connection
- Replace local artifact data with REST endpoints.
- Add curator authentication and role permissions.
- Persist notes, collections, reviews, and audit history.

## Step 5 — Advanced heritage tooling
- IIIF image manifests.
- Real 3D asset streaming.
- Annotation layers and spatial measurements.
- Versioned restoration snapshots.
